In the M1 carrier telephone system in 1925, Wolfe and co-authors identified three main development problems:
1 Understanding the line behavior:
The high-frequency behavior of power lines and related equipment had to be studied together with power companies.
2 Coupling to high-voltage lines:
A safe and efficient way was needed to connect carrier communication equipment to high-voltage conductors.
3 Developing the communication equipment:
Only after the line behavior and coupling problems were understood could the actual communication circuits and devices be designed.
